Who ACTP is

About ACTP

ACTP Sdn Bhd is listed by NSX as an International Nominated Adviser. This page sets out what NSX publishes about ACTP, with the source beside each statement.

The company

Identity and location, as listed by NSX

NSX listing

NSX's adviser list shows ACTP Sdn Bhd as an International Nominated Adviser, a member since 2022. Source: NSX adviser list (external). Status is as published by NSX and can change.

Address

NSX's ACTP profile lists the address 39, Jalan Kenari 17C, Bandar Puchong Jaya, 47100 Puchong, Selangor, Malaysia. Source: NSX adviser profile (external). ACTP is to confirm this address before it is presented as its own.

The group

ACTP and Arris Holdings Berhad

Relationship as described by NSX

NSX's ACTP profile describes ACTP Sdn. Bhd. as a wholly owned subsidiary of Arris Holdings Berhad, which it describes as NSX-listed (AR2). Source: NSX adviser profile (external).

Two separate companies

Arris Holdings Berhad publishes its own services and credentials on its own website (external). They belong to Arris and are not presented here as ACTP's own.

The relationship wording is as published by NSX and is pending confirmation by ACTP.

Limits

This site does not publish a company history, leadership or staff biographies, office locations beyond the NSX-listed address, named clients or results, because none has been confirmed for ACTP. To read the current NSX entry, see the profile above, or use the contact page.
