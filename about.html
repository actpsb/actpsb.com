Who We Are

Built on Professional Expertise

ACTP is a wholly owned subsidiary of Arris Holdings Berhad that combines expertise in corporate strategy, financial advisory, transaction support and business consulting.

Our approach combines commercial understanding, financial insight and practical execution to help businesses make informed decisions and prepare for their next stage of development. ACTP's role as a Nominated Adviser includes guiding issuers through NSX admission procedures, directors' obligations and ongoing NSX Rules guidance.
