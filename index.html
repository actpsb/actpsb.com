ACTP's listing

ACTP Sdn Bhd appears on NSX's registered Nominated Advisers list as an International adviser, a member since 2022. Source: NSX adviser list (external). Status is as published by NSX at the time of checking and can change; the NSX adviser profile (external) shows the current entry.

The nominated adviser role

NSX describes nominated advisers as helping issuers with:

  1. Admission procedures for entering the NSX market.
  2. Directors' obligations that apply to an issuer's board.
  3. Ongoing guidance on the NSX Rules.

Source: NSX nominated adviser explainer (external).

An NSX listing is not a licence, certification or endorsement of ACTP, and it does not promise that any issuer will be admitted to NSX, raise capital or achieve any outcome. This is NSX's general description of the role, not a statement of ACTP's engagement process.
