Our Specialised Expertise

NSX Nominated Adviser Services

As a recognised Nominated Adviser of the National Stock Exchange of Australia, ACTP supports issuers in navigating the admission process and understanding their responsibilities under the NSX Rules.

The Nominated Adviser role extends beyond the initial listing process, providing issuers with continuing guidance regarding relevant exchange requirements and obligations.

NSX & Your Business

The Value of an NSX Listing

A public listing can open new possibilities for a business. A nominated adviser helps its board navigate the requirements that come with that opportunity.

01 / The Market Opportunity

Access to Public Markets

Capital for the next stage
An IPO or subsequent capital raising can provide funding for growth, investment or debt repayment, subject to investor demand and applicable requirements.
Greater business visibility
A public listing can introduce a business to a wider audience of investors and potential customers through market announcements and public exposure.
A market for shareholders
Exchange trading provides a mechanism for investors to buy and sell shares and for market pricing.

Guidance at Every Stage

A clearer admission pathway
