Services

This page describes the nominated adviser role as NSX publishes it. It is not a confirmed catalogue of ACTP's engagements.

Understand the adviser role Contact

As described by NSX

The role in three parts

NSX describes a nominated adviser as helping issuers in these areas. Source: NSX nominated adviser explainer (external).

01

Admission procedures

Helping an issuer through the procedures for admission to NSX.

02

Directors' obligations

Helping an issuer's directors understand their obligations.

03

Ongoing NSX Rules guidance

Continuing guidance on the NSX Rules after admission.

Scope

Group services are separate

Not established as ACTP services

NSX's ACTP profile includes a broad services list written in a group narrative, and Arris Holdings Berhad advertises assurance, taxation and company secretarial work on its own site (external). Neither is evidence that ACTP provides those services, so they are not offered or implied here.

Describing the NSX role is not a guarantee of admission, capital raising or any outcome. ACTP's own service scope is pending confirmation by ACTP.
